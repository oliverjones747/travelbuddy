About TravelBuddy

A practical travel information resource created to help travelers research destinations, understand travel requirements and plan international journeys with greater confidence.

What Is TravelBuddy?

TravelBuddy is a travel information website focused on sharing useful resources for people planning domestic and international journeys. The website brings together destination information, travel planning ideas, visa resources, itinerary guidance and practical travel tips in one place.

Our goal is to make travel research easier by presenting information in a straightforward and accessible format. Travelers can use our resources to explore destinations, understand common travel considerations and prepare for upcoming trips.

What You Can Find on TravelBuddy

We organize travel information into several useful areas to make trip research easier.

Destination Information

Explore guides and resources covering popular destinations, attractions, travel planning and things travelers may want to consider before visiting.

Visa Resources

Find destination-specific visa resources covering common requirements, documentation and important considerations for travelers.

Travel Planning

Discover itinerary ideas, transportation information, budgeting considerations and other practical travel planning resources.

Our Approach to Travel Information

Travel planning can involve many small decisions, from choosing a destination to checking travel documents and understanding local arrangements. TravelBuddy aims to organize helpful information so visitors can research these topics more easily.

Our focus:

Clear, practical and useful travel information that helps readers make informed decisions while planning their journeys.
