30 or 60 Days in Dubai: Which Visa Makes More Sense for Your Trip?

Compare 30-day and 60-day Dubai visa options, entry types, documents, costs and application considerations before planning your trip.

Planning a Dubai trip often starts with one simple question: How long should you stay?

For some travellers, 30 days is more than enough to explore Dubai's famous landmarks, shop, relax and enjoy a few desert adventures. Others may want considerably more time because they are visiting family, combining Dubai with other UAE experiences or simply prefer a slower holiday.

This is where choosing between a 30-day and 60-day visa becomes important.

Planning a longer stay? If your plans require a longer stay, Apply 60 Days Dubai Visa can give you more flexibility than choosing a shorter-duration option. However, a longer visa is not automatically the better choice for everyone.

Here's how to decide which option makes more sense for your Dubai trip.

30-Day vs 60-Day Dubai Visa: What's the Difference?

The main difference is the permitted stay duration.

A 30-day Dubai visa is generally suited to travellers planning a conventional holiday of a few weeks. It can work well for sightseeing, shopping, short family visits or a standard Dubai vacation.

A 60-day visa provides a longer permitted stay and can be more suitable for travellers who need additional time in the UAE.

It currently states a 60-day validity and up to 60 days of stay, with the single-entry option listed at AED 750 and multiple-entry option at AED 1,799. Processing is listed as 3–4 working days.

Visa fees and processing information can change, so travellers should confirm the latest details before applying.

Who Should Choose a 30-Day Dubai Visa?

A 30-day visa may be enough if your trip has a clear and relatively short itinerary.

It can make sense for:

  • First-time Dubai visitors
  • Couples taking a holiday
  • Families planning a short vacation
  • Travellers focused mainly on Dubai
  • Shopping and leisure trips
  • Short family visits

For example, if your plan is to spend 7–15 days exploring Dubai, you probably do not need to choose a 60-day option simply because it offers a longer stay.

A shorter visa can keep your travel plans straightforward.

Who Should Apply 60 Days Dubai Visa?

A 60-day visa becomes more useful when your trip cannot comfortably fit into a few weeks.

You might consider it if you are:

  • Planning an extended family visit
  • Spending several weeks in Dubai
  • Combining different UAE destinations
  • Planning a long holiday
  • Travelling at a relaxed pace
  • Visiting friends or relatives
  • Looking for more flexibility in your travel schedule

The key question is not whether 60 days sounds better. It is whether you genuinely need that much time.

If you already know your trip will extend beyond a typical month, choosing a 60-day option from the beginning may be more practical than arranging a shorter visa and then dealing with extension requirements later.

Single Entry or Multiple Entry?

Duration is only one part of the decision. The other important question is whether you need single entry or multiple entry.

60-Day Single Entry Visa

A single-entry visa is more suitable when you plan to enter the UAE once and remain there for your trip.

For example, you could fly from India to Dubai, spend several weeks in the UAE and return home without leaving and re-entering the country.

The reference page currently lists the 60-day single-entry option at AED 750.

60-Day Multiple Entry Visa

A multiple-entry option can make more sense if your travel plans involve leaving the UAE and returning during the visa period.

For example, you might plan:

India → Dubai → another country → Dubai → India

In that situation, a multiple-entry visa can provide more flexibility than a single-entry visa.

The reference page currently lists the 60-day multiple-entry option at AED 1,799.

Always confirm the exact entry conditions attached to the visa issued to you before travelling.

How Long Do You Actually Need in Dubai?

Before choosing a visa, create a realistic travel plan.

Ask yourself:

  • How many days will I actually spend in the UAE?
  • Am I visiting only Dubai?
  • Will I visit Abu Dhabi or other emirates?
  • Am I visiting family or friends?
  • Will I leave the UAE during the trip?
  • Do I need extra flexibility?
  • Is the trip for tourism or another permitted purpose?

For a standard holiday, 30 days can be more than sufficient. For an extended stay, 60 days may make more sense.

The important point is to choose the visa according to your actual travel purpose rather than selecting the longest option simply because it gives you more time.

Documents Required for a 60-Day Dubai Visa

The exact document requirements can depend on the traveller and application channel. The reference page currently requests:

  • Passport-size photograph
  • Front page of passport
  • Last page of passport
  • Previous Dubai exit stamp, if applicable
  • Confirmed air ticket
  • Hotel voucher

It also states that the passport should have at least six months' validity and that handwritten passport formats are not accepted.

Make sure scanned documents are clear and readable. Blurry photographs, incomplete pages or inconsistent information can create unnecessary delays.

The UAE government also advises travellers to check their passport type, visa requirements and applicable entry rules before travelling.

How to Apply for a 60-Day Dubai Visa

The application process is generally straightforward when your documents are ready.

Step 1: Choose the Visa

Decide whether you need a 30-day or 60-day option and whether single or multiple entry suits your itinerary.

Step 2: Prepare Your Documents

Keep your passport copy, photograph and required travel documents ready.

Step 3: Submit the Application

Applications can be arranged through eligible UAE visa channels, including travel agents. The UAE government confirms that tourist visas can be applied for through airlines, hotels and travel agents, depending on eligibility and the relevant process.

Step 4: Wait for Processing

The reference page currently lists 3–4 working days for its 60-day visa service. This should be treated as an indicative processing time rather than a guarantee, because processing can vary.

Step 5: Check the Issued Visa

Once approved, carefully check your name, passport details, entry type and validity information before travelling.

When Should You Apply?

Do not leave your visa arrangements until the last minute.

Although the referenced service currently lists processing at 3–4 working days, actual processing can vary.

Ideally, prepare your documents and apply with enough time before your flight so that an unexpected document request or processing delay does not disrupt your travel plans.

Also check that your passport has sufficient validity before starting the application.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Before applying, avoid these common errors:

  • Choosing a 60-day visa when you only need a short stay
  • Selecting single entry when your itinerary requires re-entry
  • Uploading unclear passport scans
  • Providing inconsistent personal information
  • Ignoring passport validity requirements
  • Booking a non-refundable trip without checking visa arrangements
  • Assuming processing time is guaranteed
  • Not checking the visa after approval

Small mistakes can create unnecessary complications, so review everything carefully before submission.

Final Thoughts

So, should you choose 30 or 60 days in Dubai?

For a normal holiday lasting a few days or weeks, a 30-day visa can be perfectly practical. But if you are planning an extended stay, spending more time with family or want additional flexibility, Apply 60 Days Dubai Visa may be the more suitable choice.

Your decision should ultimately depend on your itinerary, entry requirements and how long you genuinely expect to remain in the UAE.

If you need to leave and return during your trip, also consider whether a multiple-entry option is appropriate.

Before applying, verify the current visa rules, required documents, fees and processing information. A visa that matches your actual travel plans can make the rest of your Dubai journey much easier.
